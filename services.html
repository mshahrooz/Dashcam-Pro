Questions

Frequently asked questions

How long does an install take?

A single-channel install takes about an hour; a two-channel with parking-mode battery pack takes 1.5–2 hours; a 3-channel or Tesla install can take 2–3 hours. We'll confirm your slot time when you book.

Will it drain my car's battery?

No. We use a Cellink battery pack or a low-voltage cut-off hardwire kit, so your car's 12V battery is never drawn down by parking mode. Tesla installs use the Cellink pack exclusively to avoid 12V warnings.

Can you install on a Tesla?

Yes — we're Tesla specialists. We route along factory wire channels, never tap into Tesla's proprietary harness, and use a Cellink battery pack so the 12V is untouched. Service-center friendly and fully reversible.

Do you sell the cameras too?

Yes. We carry BlackVue, VIOFO, Thinkware and Vantrue, and can recommend the right model for your vehicle and budget. You're also welcome to bring your own cam — we'll install it.

What's covered by the warranty?

Our lifetime workmanship warranty covers the installation — if our wiring ever fails, we fix it free. The dash cam hardware itself is covered by the manufacturer's warranty.
