Questions

Dash Cam Installation — FAQ

How long does installation take?

A 1-channel install takes about an hour; 2-channel with parking-mode battery takes 1.5–2 hours. We confirm your exact slot when you book.

Do you sell the cameras too?

Yes — BlackVue, VIOFO, Thinkware and Vantrue. We can recommend a model for your vehicle and budget, or install a cam you already own.

Will parking mode drain my battery?

No. We use a Cellink battery pack or a low-voltage cut-off hardwire kit, so your 12V is never drawn down. Parking mode runs cleanly for 24+ hours.

Is the install reversible?

Completely. We use factory routing and padded clips — when you sell the car we can return it to stock in under an hour.
