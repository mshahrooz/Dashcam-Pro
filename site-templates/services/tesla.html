Tesla & EV Specialists — FAQ

Will this void my Tesla warranty?

No. We don't tap into Tesla's proprietary harness or CAN bus. Power comes from a Cellink battery pack charged through an isolated circuit. Tesla service has no reason to flag the install.

Will parking mode drain my 12V?

No. Parking mode runs entirely off the Cellink NEO battery pack — your 12V is never touched. In our case study, the pack ran parking mode for 32+ hours with zero 12V warnings.

Can the installer reach the rear camera on a Model 3?

Yes. We route the rear cable along the factory headliner channel and through the existing hatch grommet — no drilling, no new holes.

Do you support Sentry mode integration?

The dash cam runs independently of Tesla's built-in Sentry/clip system. Many owners run both for redundancy — the Cellink-powered system captures continuously, including cabin, which Tesla's built-in cameras don't record.
