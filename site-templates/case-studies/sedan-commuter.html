CHALLENGE / SOLUTION
The challenge

Marcus commutes daily from Burnaby to downtown Vancouver along Hwy 1. After a parked-vehicle hit-and-run in a Metrotown parkade, the other driver claimed Marcus had rolled into them. With no witnesses and damage to both front corners, the ICBC claim stalled — and Marcus was facing his deductible and a potential at-fault rating.

The solution

We installed a BlackVue DR970X-2CH hardwired to the fuse panel with a Cellink NEO 8 battery pack for true parking mode. The front camera records 4K and the rear 1080p; both run continuously while parked for up to 24 hours off the battery pack. We routed every cable through the Civic's factory headliner and A-pillar channels — the install is invisible from the driver seat.
