CHALLENGE / SOLUTION
The challenge

Priya wanted full 360° dash cam coverage on her Model 3 but had been warned by two other shops that an install would either void her Tesla warranty or constantly drain the 12V battery — a known Model 3 pain point. She also wanted the install to be invisible enough that Tesla service wouldn't flag it during her annual inspection.

The solution

We installed a BlackVue DR970X-2CH Plus with a rear cabin-facing cam, powered exclusively off a Cellink NEO battery pack. We routed every cable along Tesla's factory headliner and glass-edge channels — no drilling, no new holes, and no taps into the proprietary harness. The Cellink pack charges through an isolated circuit, so the car's 12V battery is never touched and there are no 12V warnings.
