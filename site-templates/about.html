Our story

We started because the installs we saw weren't good enough

Burnaby DashCam Pros was founded in 2014 after our founder bought a premium dash cam from a big-box retailer and was handed a cigarette-lighter adapter and a YouTube link. The camera was excellent; the install was an afterthought — dangling wires, a drained battery, parking mode that never worked.

So we learned to do it properly: fuse-panel hardwiring, factory-channel routing, true parking mode off a Cellink battery pack, and a workmanship warranty that actually covers the work. Twelve years and 6,000+ installs later, that obsession with the wiring is still the thing that sets us apart.

Today we install every major brand — BlackVue, VIOFO, Thinkware, Vantrue — on everything from a Honda Civic to a Tesla Model S, across Burnaby and the wider Metro Vancouver area. Same shop, same standards, same lifetime workmanship warranty.

VALUES
What we stand for

Four things we won't compromise on

Hidden wiring, always

If a customer can see a cable, we did it wrong. Every install is routed through factory channels — the only sign of a dash cam is the lens behind the mirror.

Warranty that means something

A 'lifetime warranty' that excludes the things most likely to fail is marketing. Ours covers the workmanship — the part we control — for as long as you own the car.

No upsell pressure

We recommend the system that fits your car and your budget, not the one with the best margin. We'll show you exactly what each tier gets you and let you decide.

Local, not a chain

We're a Burnaby shop serving Burnaby. We know the Metro Vancouver roads, the ICBC process, and the vehicles our neighbours actually drive.
