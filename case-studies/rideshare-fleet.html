The challenge

Apex Rides operates four vehicles across Metro Vancouver. After two false rider damage claims in three months — each costing them time and an ICBC deductible — they needed cabin + road coverage that could defend drivers, be moved between vehicles, and be managed centrally.

The solution

We rolled out a VIOFO A229 Plus 3CH system (front, cabin with audio, rear) per vehicle, each on our fleet transfer kit so a cam can move between cars in five minutes. The fleet shares one cloud dashboard for live monitoring, and we supplied pre-formatted 256GB SD cards at fleet rates. Every install is heat-rated for long shifts and discreet — no visible screens while driving.
